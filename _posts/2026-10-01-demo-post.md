## Start with a question

A useful mathematical note often begins with one clear question. State the objects and assumptions first, then explain what the result says and why it matters.

> A small example can make an abstract definition easier to understand.

## Build the explanation

Use headings to guide readers through the argument. The Blog layout keeps the article text in a readable column and separates it from the title area, following Clarity’s article structure.
